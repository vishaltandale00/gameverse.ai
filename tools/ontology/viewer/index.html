GBA Game Space

Each game is a point in many views at once: how it looks, sounds and renders, what its front end does, and how a model describes its mechanics, progression and setting. Views that keep agreeing collapse into one axis. Views that disagree are independent axes, and pairs that are close on some axes and far on others are mashup seeds.
