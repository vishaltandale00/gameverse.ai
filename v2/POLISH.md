# Default polish: what every generated game gets without asking

Polish is a property of the runtime, not of the request. Every 2D game generated by v2 gets the items
below automatically. Items driven by gameplay events hang off the events the engine already emits:
`jump`, `land`, `collect`, `hurt`, `stomp`, `shoot`, `hit`, `defeated`, `reach_goal`, `time_up` and
`fell`. Style follows the design: `artStyle`, mood, tempo and palette.

## Character animation

1. **Idle:** a gentle breathing bob when standing still.
2. **Run cycle:** stride frames or procedural step bob plus forward lean, scaled by speed.
3. **Jump, fall and land:** a stretch on takeoff, a rise or fall pose, and a squash on landing.
4. **Hurt:** a knockback pose, a white flash and invulnerability blinking.
5. **Defeat and victory:** a pop-and-fade death for enemies; a player celebration at the goal.
6. **Generated pose frames:** idle, run A, run B, jump and hurt are image edits of the base sprite, so
   the character stays consistent. Procedural motion covers the gap until they're painted.

## World feel

7. **Pickups:** bob and shimmer, and pop to the HUD when collected.
8. **Enemies:** a walk wobble, a squish-flash when stomped or hit, and a burst on defeat.
9. **Goal:** a pulsing glow and a beacon when on screen.
10. **Background:** parallax with at least two depths (generated scenery plus a tinted far layer) and
    ambient drifting particles that fit the theme (snow, embers, dust, bubbles).
11. **Tiles:** generated textures with lit top edges and a soft shadow under overhangs.

## Juice

12. **Particles:** landing dust, run dust at speed, pickup sparkles, impact sparks, enemy-defeat
    debris.
13. **Screen shake** on hurt, stomp and defeat, scaled and reduced-motion aware.
14. **Hit-stop:** a 2–4 frame freeze on stomps and hits.
15. **Camera:** smoothing, look-ahead in the direction of travel, and a vertical dead-zone.

## Sound

16. **Sound effects** synthesized per game from its style: jump, land, pickup (with a rising pitch on
    streaks), hurt, stomp, shoot, hit, defeat, goal, game over, menu move and confirm.
17. **Music:** a procedural loop with tempo, key and instrumentation from the design's mood. There's
    a calmer title variant, and it ducks on pause.
18. **Mix:** master, music and effects levels; a mute toggle; it starts only after a user gesture
    (browser rule).

## Screens and flow

19. **Title screen:** generated key art, the game's title set large, the pitch, "Press Start", a
    controls card, and the title music.
20. **Level intro:** the title and a "Go!" banner, with the player dropping in.
21. **HUD:** hearts as icons, a pickup counter with the pickup's own sprite, a timer when relevant,
    and enemies defeated, styled to the game.
22. **Pause menu:** resume, restart, sound, and how to play.
23. **Game over:** the reason ("Fell into the lava"), retry, and quit to title.
24. **Victory:** a time, score and pickup tally; best times kept per game in the browser; play
    again.
25. **Transitions:** a quick fade between screens.

## Feel and fairness

26. **Input forgiveness:** coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height (already in the
    platformer module).
27. **Checkpoints:** respawn at the last safe ground after a fall when health remains, instead of a
    full restart.
28. **Controls:** keyboard plus gamepad on all screens, with on-screen hints matched to the device.
29. **Accessibility:** reduced motion turns off shake and flashes, and colors keep contrast.
30. **Performance:** 60 fps simulation, with rendering decoupled and capped at device pixel ratio 2.
