# Authored terrain and coherent recovery boundary

`src/playable-mashup-world.js` supplies the missing bounded world service for these research candidates. It is authored scene geometry, contact admission and reentry policy. Source motion remains caller-owned and unchanged; this service does not claim original SMA1 scene allocation/dispatcher closure or Kirby contact-root recovery. No ROM or private pixels are changed or published.

Canonical positions use signed Q8 (`256` units per pixel), foot-center actor origin, positive x right and positive y down. Dimensions are positive integer pixels at most 32767. Position backing must fit signed 32-bit; conversion never silently wraps or truncates. Integer mirrors are `floor(Q8 / 256)`, explicitly including negative inputs. Pixel-to-Q8 accepts integer pixels -8388608..8388607, then checks exact signed storage. This is the authored numeric boundary, not a Composer direct-storage binding; source u16/u32 or signed encodings require their own validated source boundary.

The world is complete authored rectangle geometry. `createWorld` accepts dimensions, tile size, solids, hazards, spawn and named checkpoints. `resolveTerrain(world, actor, previous, options)` consumes the caller's post-source-motion state and pre-motion Q8 position, applies x-then-y swept AABB contacts, zeroes only normal velocity, synchronizes mirrors and reports ground/landing/wall/ceiling/hazard. Each axis displacement is bounded (default 64 pixels, caller configurable 1..1024). Negative intermediate positions are supported, with horizontal and ceiling boundary clamps. Falling below the authored world is hazardous. Hazards observe the same x-then-y swept path so thin hazards cannot be skipped.

Normal motion rejects an initially penetrating actor before mutation; no general overlap depenetration or moving geometry is implemented. The service is an authored axis-ordered rectangle controller; it does not claim arbitrary continuous diagonal collision, slopes or original source frame equivalence. Actor dimensions default to half-width6 and height14 and must fit the world. Source gravity/integration cadence remains a candidate decision. `admitJump` requires a new press and grounded actor; it clears grounded immediately.

`worldToShapeProbe` explicitly maps positive authored 16px world tiles to cell row/column and local integer sample (`localY * 16 + localX`, selector1, index0..255). `requireShapeProbe` rejects direct world-coordinate binding or unsupported selector/frame/domain. The published Kirby leaf reads source shape1 signed displacement; its original physical displacement scale is unknown. This service never aliases those bytes to world pixels or claims its authored flat-rectangle contacts were recovered Kirby contacts. A candidate may query the leaf and retain its raw result without asserting physical-unit equivalence.

`resetToCheckpoint` restores both Q8 and integer coordinates, zeroes velocity, computes contact/ground and clamps/synchronizes camera in one atomic assignment. Unsafe/unknown checkpoints or invalid cameras fail before mutating actor/camera. Authored safe spawn must fit without silently changing checkpoint coordinates. This fixes the observed stale fixed-point position failure at the candidate boundary; original SMA1 retry still restores only its source integer fields and original scene initialization remains unavailable. Checkpoints do not imply durable saves or source dispatcher execution.

Exports: `createWorld`, `pixelToQ8`, `q8ToPixel`, `syncPosition`, `worldToShapeProbe`, `requireShapeProbe`, `resolveTerrain`, `admitJump`, `updateCamera`, `resetToCheckpoint`, `WORLD_POLICY_VERSION`, `Q8`.

Verification: `node --test tests/playable-mashup-world.test.js` covers negative/fractional/overflow domains, exact conversion and tile boundary, rejection of direct identity binding, ground→jump→landing, swept walls/ceiling/hazards, below-world fall, checkpoint restart/camera coherence, unsafe reset and atomic failure. Eight tests passed; final narrow runner duration 33.324583ms on the shared checkout with existing Node. This new service has no comparable before/after baseline. Queue, CI, build, rendered simulation and model evaluation are unavailable at this lane; no dependencies or CI stages were added.
