The machine-readable [capsule](../../../../local/ir/cohesive-extraction-20261016/presentation/capsule.json) contains the canonical phase graph and typed `requires`/`provides` nodes. Exact source commit and SHA256 of each dependency are in the capsule. The PNG is 144×32, 4-bit indexed, nine 16×32 cells; its 16-color palette matches the JASC palette exactly. Its delay uses the source's 6-bit field, pause and lifecycle flags retain their one-bit domains, and clip commands retain their source widths.
