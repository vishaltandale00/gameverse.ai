## Why recovery remains source-only

The original death phase is not idempotent: another admitted expired invocation loses another life. The original scene path must consume completion before calling it again. Completion 1 selects the newly qualified retry callback; completion 2 takes a distinct zero-lives path.

`08048fb0` owns disposal, mutable player-root reload, selected callback and later completion clearing. `080490b8→080adf7c` restores integer coordinates and source fields, but later scene initialization `08046a0c→080a7334→080ac9c4` reconciles fixed-point coordinates, terrain/world state and camera offsets. Checkpoint writers, full world rehydration, source animation-phase production, zero-lives continuation and persistence remain open. Those are concrete missing boundaries, not invented optional renderer services.

Blueprint contribution is `character-traversal.recovery`; `hazard-recovery.checkpoint` is source-only. Death countdown is not mapped to history rewind. All facets use the existing `sma1.controller.grounded_motion_subsystem` canonical player identity.

Native/build timings in the receipts are nested stages and must not be added again to total qualification time.
